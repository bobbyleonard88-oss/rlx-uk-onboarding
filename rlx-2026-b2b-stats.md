# RLX 2026 — B2B Sales Collateral: Headline Stats & Sponsor Quotes

*Based on 278 curated 1:1 meetings across 21 sponsors and 38 senior TA leaders, RLX UK, March 2026.*

---

These are the stats that convert. Lead with these in proposals, decks, and follow-up emails.

## Stat Clusters for Different Contexts

### For a sales email or LinkedIn post
> 56% of curated 1:1 meetings at RLX 2026 were rated as active pipeline by sponsors. Not leads. Not conversations. Pipeline.

### For a proposal deck slide
> **278 curated meetings. 38 senior TA leaders. 80% average AI match score. 1 in 2 meetings = immediate pipeline.**

### For an ROI conversation
> Sponsors rated delegate seniority **4.3 out of 5**. The 1:1 format **4.25 out of 5**. And 82% said they want to come back in October.

### For overcoming the "is it worth it?" objection
